Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is DocuXray compared to manual data entry?

▼

DocuXray delivers 99%+ accuracy on document extraction, which significantly outperforms manual data entry (typically 96-98% accurate due to human error). Our AI double-checks its own work and flags inconsistencies automatically, so your team spends less time reviewing and correcting data. Unlike manual entry, DocuXray maintains consistent accuracy even as document volume scales.

Do I need to train the AI for my specific document formats?

▼

No training required! DocuXray works out of the box with any document format. Whether you're processing invoices from multiple vendors, receipts in different layouts, or custom forms, our AI adapts automatically. There's no need for templates, manual mapping, or rule-building — just upload and extract. The system learns and improves as it processes more documents.

What file types and languages does DocuXray support?

▼

DocuXray handles PDFs, scanned images (JPG, PNG), handwritten notes, and multilingual documents seamlessly. We support documents in English, Hindi, and major Indian regional languages, as well as international languages. Whether your documents are typed, scanned, photographed, or handwritten, DocuXray extracts clean data from all formats in one unified workflow.

How does the 100 free pages per month work?

▼

Every new account gets 100 free pages per month with no credit card required. You can process any type of document — invoices, receipts, contracts, statements — and extract data instantly. If you need more than 100 pages, you can upgrade to a paid plan anytime. Unused free pages don't roll over, but you'll get a fresh 100 pages at the start of each month.

Can DocuXray integrate with Tally, QuickBooks, or my existing accounting software?

▼

Yes! DocuXray offers direct integration with Tally, QuickBooks, and other popular accounting platforms. You can sync extracted data with one click, eliminating manual re-entry. Our API also allows you to integrate DocuXray into your existing workflows and custom systems. If you need a specific integration, our team can help set it up for you.

Is my financial data secure and compliant with data privacy regulations?

▼

Absolutely. DocuXray uses enterprise-grade security with end-to-end encryption to protect your sensitive financial data. We're compliant with industry standards including GDPR and SOC 2, and we never share your data with third parties. Your documents are processed securely and stored with bank-level encryption.
